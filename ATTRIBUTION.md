# Attribution & licences

The Parallel Reader mixes **original writing** with **openly-licensed and public-domain German texts**. This file records where each imported text came from and under what terms, as those licences require. Attribution is also shown in the app itself, underneath every text.

Nothing in this repo is reproduced without a licence that permits it.

---

The 30 texts in the categories **Alltag, Gespräche, Land & Leute, Geschichten, Wissen** and **Meinung** were written for this project. So were all English translations throughout, including those accompanying the imported texts below, and all grammar notes.

## Public domain — no licence required

Two texts in the **🎭 Klassiker** category are complete short tales from the Brothers Grimm, *Kinder- und Hausmärchen*, 7th edition (Göttingen: Dieterich, 1857), via [German Wikisource](https://de.wikisource.org/).

| Text | Reference |
|---|---|
| Der süße Brei | KHM 103 — [Wikisource](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Der_s%C3%BC%C3%9Fe_Brei_(1857)) |
| Die Sternthaler | KHM 153 — [Wikisource](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Die_Sternthaler_(1857)) |

Jacob Grimm died in 1863 and Wilhelm Grimm in 1859, so these texts have been in the public domain worldwide for well over a century. They are reproduced in the 1857 orthography (`gieng`, `wußte`, `Armuth`, `Thaler`), which is why they are filed at B2 rather than lower.

## What is deliberately *not* here

No contemporary copyrighted material has been copied into this repo — no newspaper articles, no modern short stories, no textbook content, no broadcaster transcripts. Those sources are **linked** from the Library tab instead.

This is a licensing decision, but also a practical one: a repo full of scraped copies goes stale and breaks, whereas links and openly-licensed text do not.

For full-length German literature, the Library links to [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/browse/languages/de) and [LibriVox](https://librivox.org/), where the public-domain canon is available in its entirety and legitimately downloadable.
